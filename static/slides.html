The Solution

SautiCare: Pan-African Oral Voice Assistant

Respectful, localized voice guidance in 4 major African languages with +40% elder audio boost

🌍 4 Regional Linguistic Spheres

• East Africa (Kiswahili): Kenya & Tanzania (Zuri, Rafiki, Daudi, Rehema)
• West Africa (Nigerian Pidgin): Nigeria (Ezinne, Abeo)
• Southern Africa (isiZulu): South Africa (Thando, Themba)
• Horn of Africa (Amharic): Ethiopia (Mekdes, Ameha)

👵 Culturally Grounded Honorifics

Built on Heshima kwa Wazee (Respect for Elders). Greets elders warmly (*"Shikamoo Mzee/Mama"*, *"Kedu Elder"*, *"Sawubona Mkhulu"*, *"ጤና ይስጥልኝ አያቴ"*). Speaks calmly with slower cadence and amplified acoustics.

📱 Visual Confirmation & OS Deep-Linking

Every voice interaction produces high-contrast, step-by-step visual cards and direct OS actions: launching WhatsApp, dialing USSD, or enlarging system fonts.

📶 Zero-Data Rural Resilience (PWA)

When mobile data drops to 0MB, SautiCare automatically falls back to hardware GSM dialing and pre-cached offline guides—guaranteeing 24/7 lifeline access.

Voice Font Magnifier

Safe Mobile Money

Guides USSD execution for M-Pesa (*334#), OPay, and Telebirr while actively cautioning elders to never reveal their PIN.

Camera & Grandchildren Photos

Clear instructions on holding the phone steady and tapping the round white shutter button to capture and save memories.

🚨

One-Tap Emergency SOS

Instantly dispatches an SMS with live GPS coordinates to the elder's child and triggers a direct GSM phone call.
